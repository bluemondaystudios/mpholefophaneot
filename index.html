Meet Mpho

Therapy built around real life.

Mpho Lefophane is a qualified Occupational Therapist with a Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (B.Occ) from Sefako Makgatho Health Sciences University, registered with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA). She is passionate about helping people of all ages build independence, improve their quality of life and take part in the activities that matter most to them.

Her approach is holistic and client-centred, recognising that every person has unique needs, strengths, goals and circumstances.

Services

Support for different stages of life.

A broad occupational therapy offering, with care tailored to the individual rather than a one-size-fits-all programme.

Infant & Early Intervention

Support for developmental delays, premature birth follow-up and delayed milestones in infants and young children.

See details
  • Developmental delays
  • Premature birth follow-up
  • Delayed milestones (rolling, sitting, crawling, walking)

School Assessments & Learning Difficulties

Assessments for children who struggle with handwriting, concentration, coordination or school readiness.

See details

Who can benefit? Children who:

  • Struggle with handwriting or drawing
  • Have difficulty concentrating or completing schoolwork
  • Find reading, writing or copying from the board challenging
  • Have poor coordination or fine motor skills
  • Experience sensory processing difficulties that affect learning
  • Are not meeting expected developmental or academic milestones
  • Need a school readiness assessment before starting Grade R or Grade 1
  • Have been referred by a teacher, doctor, psychologist or other healthcare professional

Home Modifications

A practical review of the home to make everyday spaces safer, more accessible and better suited to individual needs.

See details

Review of key areas, including:

  • Entrance and exits
  • Living room
  • Kitchen
  • Bathroom
  • Bedroom
  • Passageways
  • Outdoor pathways

Mental Health Rehabilitation

Individual, group, family and couples support for a wide range of mental health conditions.

See details

Conditions supported:

  • Anxiety disorders
  • Depression
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)
  • Stress-related conditions and burnout
  • Adjustment disorders
  • Mental health challenges associated with chronic illness or physical disability

Individual therapy

  • Improve emotional regulation and coping strategies
  • Manage stress and anxiety
  • Build confidence and self-esteem
  • Improve concentration, organisation and problem-solving
  • Increase independence in daily activities
  • Return to work, school or meaningful occupations
  • Improve social participation and quality of life

Group programmes

  • Stress and anxiety management
  • Emotional regulation and coping skills
  • Life, social and communication skills development
  • Self-care, wellness, mindfulness and relaxation
  • Building confidence and resilience
  • Vocational and community reintegration

Family support

  • Improve understanding of mental health conditions
  • Enhance communication within the family
  • Address changes in family roles and responsibilities

Couples support

  • Improve communication and problem-solving
  • Navigate changes in daily routines and responsibilities
  • Adapt to illness, disability or caregiving roles
  • Develop strategies for managing stress together
  • Promote healthy participation in shared, meaningful occupations

Assistive Devices

Assessment, ordering and issuing of assistive devices tailored to individual needs.

See details

Recommendations may include:

  • Mobility aids
  • Bathroom & toileting equipment
  • Bedroom equipment
  • Daily living aids
  • Seating & positioning equipment
  • Paediatric equipment

Functional Capacity & Medico-Legal

Functional capacity evaluations and medico-legal reporting for relevant cases.

Physical Rehabilitation

Support following stroke, traumatic brain injuries, motor vehicle accidents and other conditions affecting function and independence.

Paediatric Rehabilitation
